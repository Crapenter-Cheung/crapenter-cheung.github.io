  <div class="talk-card__row">
    <h3 class="talk-card__title">
      <a href="{{ '/assets/schur_positivity_nabla.pdf' | relative_url }}" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">
        Schur positivity and the nabla operator
      </a>
    </h3>
    <span class="talk-card__date">November 13, 2026</span>
  </div>

  <p class="talk-card__venue">
    <a href="https://sites.google.com/view/cart-kias/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">
      CART Seminar (KIAS, PKU, SKKU)
    </a>.
  </p>

  <details>
    <summary>Abstract</summary>
    <p>
      I will discuss several Schur positivity conjectures posed by Bergeron,
      Garsia, Haiman, and Tesler for the action of \( \nabla \) on monomial
      symmetric functions and modified Hall&ndash;Littlewood polynomials.
      The monomial case follows by combining expansions in the \( C \)-operators
      with the compositional shuffle theorem. For the Hall&ndash;Littlewood case,
      differences associated with covers in dominance order can be expressed
      through LLT polynomials; Catalanimals and a Cauchy identity then give
      the required positivity. I will also explain how a \( C \)-operator
      expansion of \( p_n \), together with combinatorial transformations,
      leads to a rational extension of Sergel&rsquo;s square paths theorem
      involving \( (km,kn) \)-preference functions.
      The results come from joint work with Dun Qiu and, for part of the talk,
      with Feng Gu and Mingyang Kang.
    </p>
  </details>
</div>

<div class="pub-card talk-card">
  <div class="talk-card__row">
    <h3 class="talk-card__title">
      <a href="{{ '/assets/talk2_nonsymmetric_delta_slides.pdf' | relative_url }}" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">
        The nonsymmetric compositional Delta conjecture
      </a>
    </h3>
    <span class="talk-card__date">July 29, 2026</span>
  </div>

  <p class="talk-card__venue">
    <a href="https://sites.google.com/view/skkucombinatorics/home" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">
      SKKU Combinatorics Seminar
    </a>, Sungkyunkwan University.
  </p>

  <details>
    <summary>Abstract</summary>
    <p>
      The compositional Delta theorem is a refinement of the compositional shuffle theorem.
      In this talk, I will present a nonsymmetric version of the compositional Delta theorem.
      Using flagged LLT polynomials, we establish both signed and unsigned nonsymmetric
      identities. We also introduce nonsymmetric analogues of the operators \( \nabla \)
      and \( \tau^* \), leading to a new operator formulation of these identities. Under
      Weyl symmetrization, our nonsymmetric identities recover the original compositional
      Delta theorem, revealing the symmetric theory as a natural consequence of the
      nonsymmetric framework. I will conclude with several conjectural positivity
      properties involving stable atoms. This is joint work with Dun Qiu.
    </p>
  </details>
</div>

<div class="pub-card talk-card">
  <div class="talk-card__row">
    <h3 class="talk-card__title">
      <a href="{{ '/assets/talk1_signed_schur_reference_style.pdf' | relative_url }}" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">
        Signed Schur positivity of \( \nabla m_\mu \)
      </a>
    </h3>
    <span class="talk-card__date">July 27, 2026</span>
  </div>

  <p class="talk-card__venue">
    <a href="https://sites.google.com/view/skkucombinatorics/home" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">
      SKKU Combinatorics Seminar
    </a>, Sungkyunkwan University.
  </p>

  <details>
    <summary>Abstract</summary>
    <p>
      The \( \nabla \) operator plays a central role in the theory of Macdonald polynomials
      and diagonal coinvariants. In 1999, Bergeron, Garsia, Haiman, and Tesler conjectured
      that, up to a natural sign, \( \nabla m_\mu \) is Schur-positive, where \( m_\mu \)
      is the monomial symmetric function. In this talk, I will present a proof of this
      conjecture, together with a generalization in the setting of the Schiffmann algebra.
      A key ingredient is a positive expansion of signed monomial symmetric functions in
      terms of the functions \( C_\alpha(1) \). Combined with the compositional shuffle
      theorem and the Schur positivity of LLT polynomials, this expansion yields the desired
      result. I will also discuss a related \( e \)-positivity phenomenon arising from
      column LLT polynomials. This is joint work with Dun Qiu.
    </p>
  </details>
</div>
